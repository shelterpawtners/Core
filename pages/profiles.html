Digital Pet Profiles

We digitize shelter "Pet Report Cards" into secure profiles that follow each pet—so adopters, shelters, and vets can make faster, better decisions with fewer repeat tests.

🐾 Create Profile 🏥 Request Shelter Demo ⚕️ Join as a Vet

What's Included in Every Digital Pet Profile

🩺

Medical History

  • Vaccinations and schedules
  • Current medications
  • Known allergies and reactions
  • Health conditions and treatments
  • Spay/neuter status
🐕

Behavioral Insights

  • Known commands and tricks
  • Likes and dislikes
  • Training level and progress
  • Exercise needs and preferences
  • Behavioral notes and triggers
🏠

Compatibility Data

  • Good with children (ages)
  • Compatibility with other pets
  • Living situation preferences
  • Activity level requirements
  • Social interaction needs
🆔

Identification & Care

  • Microchip ID and registration
  • Current photos and markings
  • Feeding preferences and schedule
  • Grooming needs and frequency
  • Emergency contact information

Scan & Share with Owner Consent

Your pet's profile is accessible anywhere you need it—emergency rooms, boarding facilities, travel—with complete owner control.

📱

Microchip Scanning

Vets can scan your pet's existing microchip to instantly access their complete Digital Pet Profile with your consent.

📱

QR Code Access

Show a secure QR code on your phone to instantly share relevant profile information with authorized care providers.

🛡️

RFID Security

Advanced RFID technology provides secure, contactless access to profile data for emergency situations and routine care.

Benefits for Everyone

🏠

For Adopters

Faster Care

No more repeating medical history at every vet visit. Your pet's complete record is instantly available.

Peace of Mind

Emergency-ready profiles mean better care decisions when every minute counts.

Better Training

Behavioral insights help create customized training plans that work for your specific pet.

Fewer Tests

Complete vaccination and medical history reduces unnecessary duplicate testing and procedures.

🏢

For Shelters

Always Free

All Digital Pet Profile tools and ShelterCARD programs are completely free for shelters forever.

Better Matches

Complete profiles help families find the right pet, leading to more successful adoptions.

Fewer Returns

When families know what to expect, they're better prepared for successful pet ownership.

Staff Time Saved

Guided intake forms reduce paperwork and ensure consistent, complete information capture.

⚕️

For Veterinarians

Complete History

See the full medical and behavioral history from day one of the first visit.

Faster Triage

Known allergies, medications, and conditions help prioritize care and avoid dangerous interactions.

Reduced Guesswork

Behavioral insights and compatibility notes inform treatment approaches and handling techniques.

Better Outcomes

Complete information leads to more accurate diagnoses and more effective treatment plans.

Privacy & Security Promise

Your pet's data is protected with enterprise-grade security and you maintain complete control over who can access it.

Owner Controlled Consent

You decide who can access your pet's profile and what information they can see. Grant or revoke access anytime, anywhere.

Minimal Data Sharing

Only relevant information is shared with authorized parties. Emergency contacts see emergency info, groomers see grooming notes.

Complete Audit Trail

See exactly who accessed your pet's profile, when they accessed it, and what information they viewed.

Enterprise Encryption

All data is encrypted at rest and in transit using industry-standard security protocols. Your pet's information is always protected.
