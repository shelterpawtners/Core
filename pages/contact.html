Quick Answers to Common Questions

How do I reset my password?

Click "Sign In" and then "Forgot Password." We'll send you a reset link via email.

How long does verification take?

Pet owner verification typically takes 24-48 hours. Business applications are reviewed within 2-3 business days.

Can I add multiple pets?

Yes! You can add as many rescued pets as you have to your account, each with their own profile and benefits.

Is there a mobile app?

Our mobile app is coming soon! For now, our website is fully mobile-responsive and works great on all devices.

How do I report a technical issue?

Use the contact form above with "Technical Issue" as the subject, or email support@shelterpawtners.org directly.

Do you offer shelter partnerships?

Absolutely! Contact us at shelters@shelterpawtners.org to discuss how we can partner with your organization.
