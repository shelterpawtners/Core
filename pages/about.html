About Shelter Pawtners

We're on a mission to ensure every rescued pet receives the love and care they deserve through lifetime savings and community support.

Our Story

Shelter Pawtners was founded in 2020 when a group of animal lovers and local business owners came together with a simple question: "How can we make quality pet care more accessible for shelter-adopted pets?"

The answer was a platform that connects compassionate pet owners with businesses who want to give back to the rescue community. What started as a small pilot program in one city has grown into a nationwide movement.

Today, we're proud to serve thousands of rescued pets and their families, partnering with hundreds of businesses who believe in our mission.

Our Vision

A world where every shelter pet has access to affordable, quality care for their entire life, and where businesses and communities rally together to support animal welfare.

Our Mission

To create a sustainable ecosystem that rewards pet owners who adopt from shelters, empowers businesses to make a difference, and strengthens the entire rescue community.

The Problem We Solve

💔

High Pet Care Costs

Many pet owners struggle with the costs of veterinary care, grooming, and supplies, sometimes leading to difficult choices about their pet's health.

🏥

Limited Post-Adoption Support

After adoption, many pet owners feel alone in managing their pet's care, medical records, and ongoing needs.

🤝

Disconnected Communities

Businesses want to support rescue animals, but lack a structured way to connect with and reward adopters.

Our Solution

For Pet Owners

  • Lifetime discounts on pet care services
  • Centralized pet profile and medical records
  • Microchip registration and management
  • Access to a supportive community
  • Easy verification of adoption status

For Businesses

  • Positive brand association with animal welfare
  • Access to loyal, engaged customers
  • Simple platform to manage discount programs
  • Marketing support and promotion
  • Community recognition and visibility

For Shelters

  • Better post-adoption outcomes
  • Reduced return rates
  • Strengthened community partnerships
  • Tracking of adopted pets' wellbeing
  • Enhanced adoption appeal

For the Community

  • Increased adoption rates
  • Better pet health outcomes
  • Stronger local business connections
  • Reduced shelter overcrowding
  • Celebration of rescue success stories
