Business FAQs

How do I verify Shelter Pawtners members?

Members show their digital or physical ID card. You can scan the QR code or enter their member number in our verification portal to confirm active membership.

Can I change my discount or services later?

Yes! You can update your discount offering, services, and profile information anytime through your business dashboard. We recommend 30 days notice for changes.

What marketing support do you provide?

All partners receive window decals and digital badges. Featured and Premium partners get email promotions, social media features, and custom marketing materials.

How do I track my business impact?

Your dashboard shows member visits, total discounts given, customer reviews, average rating, and other key metrics to track your community impact.
