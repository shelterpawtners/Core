How ShelterPAWtners Works

A simple, secure platform connecting rescued pets with lifetime savings

For Pet Owners

1 Create Your Account

Sign up for free and create your pet's profile. Include your pet's name, microchip number, and adoption information from your shelter or rescue organization.

2 Get Verified

We'll verify your pet's adoption status with the shelter. This usually takes 24-48 hours. Once verified, you'll receive your ShelterPAWtners ID card (digital and physical).

3 Browse Partners

Explore our directory of participating businesses in your area. Filter by service type, location, and discount level to find the best deals for your pet.

4 Save Money

Show your ShelterPAWtners ID at participating businesses to receive your discount. Track your savings and review your favorite partners in your account dashboard.

5 Manage Records

Keep all your pet's veterinary records, vaccination history, and medical information organized in one secure place. Access them anytime, anywhere.

6 Share the Love

Refer friends who've adopted pets and earn bonus perks. Share your success stories and help grow the rescue community.

For Businesses

1 Apply to Join

Complete our simple business application. Tell us about your services and what discount you'd like to offer to ShelterPAWtners members.

2 Get Approved

Our team reviews your application to ensure quality standards. Once approved, you'll receive your partner welcome kit and marketing materials.

3 List Your Business

Create your business profile with photos, services, and discount details. Your listing appears in our directory and is promoted to local pet owners.

4 Welcome Members

Use our simple verification system to confirm membership and apply discounts. Provide exceptional service and build lasting relationships.

5 Track Impact

Access your business dashboard to see member visits, total discounts given, customer reviews, and your community impact metrics.

6 Grow Together

Benefit from our marketing campaigns, participate in community events, and gain loyal customers who value businesses that support rescue animals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a fee to join as a pet owner?

No! ShelterPAWtners is completely free for pet owners. We believe rescued pets deserve access to affordable care, and we never want cost to be a barrier.

What documents do I need to verify my pet's adoption?

You'll need your adoption paperwork from the shelter or rescue organization, and your pet's microchip number. If you don't have these, contact your shelter—they can help!

Can I add multiple pets to my account?

Absolutely! You can add as many rescued pets as you have. Each pet gets their own profile and qualifies for the same lifetime benefits.

How do discounts work?

Each business sets their own discount level (typically 10-30% off regular prices). Simply show your ShelterPAWtners ID when paying to receive your discount.

What types of businesses can join?

We welcome veterinary clinics, groomers, pet supply stores, trainers, boarders, pet sitters, and any business that serves pets and wants to support the rescue community.

Is there a cost for businesses to participate?

We offer flexible partnership tiers. Basic listing is free, and premium options provide enhanced visibility and marketing support. Contact us for details.

How do I verify ShelterPAWtners members?

Members show their digital or physical ID card. You can scan the QR code or enter their member ID in our verification system to confirm active membership.

Can I change my discount level?

Yes! You can adjust your discount offering at any time through your business dashboard. We recommend giving 30 days notice to members.

Still have questions? We're here to help!
