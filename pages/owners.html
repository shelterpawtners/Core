Complete Digital Pet Profile accessible anywhere, anytime

Never wonder about your pet's vaccination history, medication allergies, or behavioral quirks again. Your Digital Pet Profile keeps everything organized and accessible—perfect for vet visits, emergencies, boarding, or travel. Your pet's complete story travels with them wherever they go.

Lifelong savings with ShelterCARD on real-world care

Exclusive discounts for adopted pets only

Your choice to adopt deserves to be rewarded. ShelterCARD provides exclusive, lifelong discounts on veterinary care, premium nutrition, professional grooming, training classes, boarding services, and more. Because adopted pets deserve the very best care at prices that work for your family.

Critical information available when seconds count

When emergencies happen, every second counts. Your pet's Digital Pet Profile provides instant access to critical information—known allergies, current medications, emergency contacts, and behavioral notes. Whether it's a late-night ER visit or last-minute boarding, the information is always there when you need it most.
