What You'll Need:

  • Your contact information
  • Your pet's basic details (name, species, breed)
  • Adoption paperwork or shelter name
  • Your pet's microchip number (if available)
Note: If you don't have all the information now, you can create your account and add details later. Your microchip number can be found in your adoption paperwork or by contacting your veterinarian.

What Happens After You Sign Up?

Day 1: Verification

We'll contact your shelter to verify your pet's adoption. Most verifications complete within 24-48 hours. You'll receive an email update.

Day 2-3: Activation

Once verified, your account activates and you receive your digital ID card. Your physical card ships within 5-7 business days.
