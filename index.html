Helping Shelter Pets Thrive After Adoption

Why We Exist

Every year, millions of healthy pets are euthanized simply because shelters run out of space. We believe that when someone chooses to adopt, they deserve all the support we can give them—forever.

📊

Better Veterinary Care

When your pet visits the vet, their complete medical history is instantly available. No more repeating information or risking dangerous drug interactions.

💳

Meaningful Savings

Because you chose to adopt, you get lifetime discounts on everything your pet needs—from vet visits to grooming to training classes.

🔬

Advancing Pet Health

Your pet's anonymized health data helps researchers discover new treatments and preventive care methods that benefit all animals.

How It Works

🏠

1. Shelter Creates Profile

Animal shelters create complete digital profiles for every pet, including medical history, personality traits, and care needs before adoption.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

2. Transfer to Adopter

When you adopt, the shelter transfers your pet's complete digital profile to you, giving you instant access to all their information.

💳

3. ShelterCARD Benefits

Your ShelterCARD connects to the microchip and unlocks lifetime savings at vets, pet stores, and services in our caring network.
