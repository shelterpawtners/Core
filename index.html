How It Works

1. Sign Up

Create your free account and add your rescued pet's profile, including microchip number and adoption information.

2. Get Verified

We verify your pet's adoption status with your shelter to ensure authenticity and build trust with our business partners.

3. Start Saving

Access lifetime discounts at participating businesses. Show your Shelter Pawtners ID and enjoy the savings!
